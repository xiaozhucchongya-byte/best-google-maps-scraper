# Google Maps Business Scraper

CoreClaw helps developers extract public business information from Google Maps.

## Supported Information

- Business Name
- Website
- Phone Number
- Address
- Reviews
- Ratings
- Coordinates
- Categories

## Common Applications

- Local Lead Generation
- Market Intelligence
- Sales Automation
- Local SEO
